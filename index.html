About the Lab

The Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Systems (AIIS) Laboratory at California State University, Fresno conducts interdisciplinary research in artificial intelligence and intelligent systems, with an emphasis on developing AI technologies with scientific and societal impact.

  • Machine Learning & Intelligent Decision-Making: deep learning, generative AI, reinforcement learning, optimization, intelligent control, and multiagent systems.
  • AI for Sustainable Cyber-Physical Systems: climate-smart agriculture, energy and smart buildings, transportation, scientific computing, sensing, and the Internet of Things.
  • AI, Society & Human Agency: human–AI interaction, explainability, trustworthy AI, education, public history, and the societal implications of AI and automation.

Selected Projects

  • Hibiki-AI : a generative-AI platform for source-grounded, interactive historical learning based on Japanese American incarceration testimony.
  • CIWA & CIWA+ : machine learning, computer vision, and multimodal sensing for intelligent precision irrigation and crop water-stress estimation.
  • CompactAI : hybrid machine-learning surrogate modeling for rapid scientific and engineering simulation.
  • Agr-IoT: low-cost, expandable, open-source Internet of Things infrastructure for irrigation automation and sustainable agriculture.
  • ROLLAM: research on improving and evaluating the robustness of large language models.
